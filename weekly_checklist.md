# Weekly human check

The script sees yields, auctions, and prices in hours. These reads catch the *why* before it is fully in the tape.

Do this once a week, same day, same sitting. Fifteen minutes.

## 1. Japan flows
- [PIIE — Robin Brooks](https://www.piie.com/experts/robin-brooks)
- [Brad Setser, CFR](https://www.cfr.org/expert/brad-w-setser) / [Follow the Money](https://www.cfr.org/blog/brad-setser)
- Look for: life-insurer / GPIF / MoF comments on UST allocation, official-sector selling, "home bias" in JGBs.

## 2. Treasury buybacks
- [Quarterly refunding](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financing-the-government/quarterly-refunding)
- [TreasuryDirect buybacks](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/auctions/buybacks/)
- Look for: larger size, higher frequency, more long-end operations. That is Bessent capping the 30-year.

## 3. Bank of Japan
- [Policy board minutes](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/mpmsche_minu/index.htm)
- Look for: "financial stability", JGB market functioning, any hint they will not fully backstop 30Y/40Y.

## 4. Spec positioning
- [CFTC Commitments of Traders](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/index.htm)
- Look for: crowded long gold, crowded short Treasuries. Crowding makes the gap violent when it comes.

## 5. Auction calendar (next 10 days)
- [TreasuryDirect upcoming](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/auctions/upcoming/)
- Cross-check the dashboard "Upcoming" table. A 10-year or 30-year on a week when JGB 40Y is ripping is the session that matters.

## 6. BTC plumbing
- [Glassnode exchange balance](https://studio.glassnode.com/metrics?a=BTC&m=distribution.BalanceExchanges) / [CryptoQuant exchange reserve](https://cryptoquant.com/asset/btc/chart/exchange-flows/exchange-reserve)
- [SoSoValue US BTC ETF](https://sosovalue.com/assets/etf/us-btc-spot) / [Farside IBIT](https://farside.co.uk/BTC/)
- [Strategy ledger](https://www.strategy.com/ledger) — mNAV and QTD BTC acquired. Negative QTD means they sold.

## How to read a print
| Print | Soft | Danger | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bid-to-cover (10Y/30Y) | < 2.2x | < 2.0x | < 1.8x or undersubscribed |
| Tail (high − median yield) | 3–5 bps | > 5 bps | gap + failed cover |
| Indirects (foreign) | fading | collapse | n/a |

Indirect bidder share falling alongside cover is the Japan-flow tell that Setser writes about.
